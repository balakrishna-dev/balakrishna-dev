## 👤 About

I am a NetSuite techno-functional consultant who works on both sides of a project. I gather requirements and configure the business process, then build the SuiteScript and integrations myself. When a solution needs to live outside NetSuite, I build the supporting services in **Python (Django DRF)** or **PHP (CodeIgniter)**.

I support implementations end to end: requirements, configuration, customisation, data migration, UAT, go-live and post-go-live support, and I build everything to stay maintainable after handover.
