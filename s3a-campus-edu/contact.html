FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Book a demo — we reply within one business hour.

How long does it take to implement S3A Campus EDU?

Most schools go live within 7 days. Our team handles configuration, data migration and staff training for you.

Can you migrate data from our existing software or Excel?

Yes. We import student, staff, fee and balance data from Excel sheets or most existing school software at no extra cost.

Is our data safe?

All data is encrypted in transit with SSL, protected with role-based access, and backed up automatically every day on secure cloud servers.

Do parents get a mobile app?

Yes — Android and iOS apps for parents, students, teachers and management. We can also publish a branded app with your school's name and logo.

Can we choose only the modules we need?

Absolutely. Start with the core modules and add transport, HR, LMS, hostel or others whenever you're ready.

What kind of support do you provide?

Phone, WhatsApp, email and remote-screen support, plus on-site visits for training. Larger institutions and multi-branch groups get a dedicated account manager.
